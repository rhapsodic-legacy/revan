## 1. The setting

### The Bright Gyre
A spiral galaxy of about ten thousand settled worlds. Its political heart is **the Galactic Unity**: the hundred dense Core worlds (**the Hundred**), each sovereign and each with its own defense fleet, bound by an assembly that is **autocratic, corrupt, and drowning in bureaucracy**: votes are bought, committees outlive wars, and many **Unity Guardsmen** are little more than rent-a-goons in nice armour. The player only learns how rotten it is gradually. Past the rim lies **the Black Between**, the starless gulf between galaxies. Charts say there is nothing on the other side.

### Eternity and Seers
Beneath time runs a current called **Eternity**. Those who perceive it are **Seers**, and each perceives it differently:
- **Dust**: the **Weave**, threads and the dark gaps between them. He can pull the gaps open.
- **Ashe**: a few heartbeats ahead.
- **Melonsi**: **Silk**, the threads that bind people. She can tie and pull them.
- **The Emperor**: *ownership*. Everything Eternity touches is food.

Seers **kindle** their ornate swords by will, wrapping the blade in a crackling field that cuts any matter. Flame colors: **dawnfire** gold (Srov), **wrathfire** red-violet (Vors), **starless white** (Akulsa alone), **gravefire** black with a sick green rim (the Empire). Strong Seers age slowly. The very strongest barely age at all.

### The four peoples and their sound
| Faction | Look | Sound | Creed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Unity / everyone else** | Chrome, wedge ships with flip-up headlights, magenta and cyan neon, CRT holograms, cassette "tapes" as data, arcades, rinks, rain-slick slums under neon | 70s–80s synthwave | "Keep the lights on." |
| **The Srov** | Salt-cliff temples, sand robes, lacquered curved swords with silk cords, ash face-lines | Wild mystical desert ritual: wordless choirs, throat singing, frame drums, reeds | **Stillness.** Some things should be forgotten. |
| **The Vors** | Gothic cathedral-fortresses, black iron and crimson, cathedral-hilted greatswords, rank masks, Juggernaut-class battlecruisers | Grand full-orchestra march | **Burn.** Remember everything, and spend it. |
| **The Immortal Empire** | Black glass, bone, verdigris, green candle-flame, black visor-masks with white pinprick eyes | Dirge: low choir, bells, drone | **Ownership.** |

### The Unity's flaw
Every Unity world keeps its own fleet for its own skies. Nobody gives battleships to anyone. When Akulsa's Juggernauts came, the Unity's answer was citizen captains in smuggler craft with stolen laser cannons. **A hundred worlds fell in less than a hundred days.** That is still true today: the Srov beg worlds to lend battleships to hunt the rogue Vors fleets, and most worlds refuse. Rogue fleets haven't attacked *them*.

## 2. True history

**~4,000 years ago.** Beyond the Black Between, a king traded the Unsleeping (gods that feed on Eternity) his name, his warmth, and his firstborn for deathlessness. He became **Emperor Voidreaper** of the **Immortal Empire of the Ever-Throne**, and fed his own galaxy to them until it was a graveyard called **the Pale**.

**~3,000 years ago.** A circle of the Emperor's Seers fled into the Gyre, sealed the one navigable route (**the Narrows**), and founded the **Srov** on one rule: *the Empire must be forgotten. A forgotten god starves.* From this came the Srov's most sacred art: **the Unremembering**, taking memory from a mind. The Srov have enforced the Empire's death ever since, and most Srov believe it.

**~1,000 years ago.** The **Vors** were destroyed by the Srov in the Quiet War. Their throneworld **Tal Vorrun** and their Juggernauts were sealed and forgotten. Vors lore preserved one rite: **the Succession**, in which a dying Grandmaster pours his memories into his apprentice so the order never truly loses a master. *"A Vors does not die. He passes into the heir."*

**~35 years ago.** A self-taught Seer from the docks found Tal Vorrun's tombs and took the title **Vors Akulsa**. He crossed the Black Between alone, found the Pale and the Ever-Throne, saw the Empire's fleets readying to cross, and read the Emperor's own Seers' prophecy: *blood cannot be fed to blood; only his own blood can unmake him.*

**~18 years ago.** Akulsa stole the Emperor's blood: the infant heir, carried out by his mother **Ysolde**, a sacrifice-bride, who held the snow-gate so Akulsa could run. The Emperor cut her down in the snow. Akulsa named the boy **Tyranus**.

**~18–13 years ago.** The Srov Council refused Akulsa's warning. He unsealed the Juggernauts and rebuilt the Vors. He raised Tyranus as a child-apprentice, a prodigy who could kindle a blade at four. The child's flame was smoky gold with a black heart. Akulsa took in other war-orphans (among them **Sable Nox**) and a brilliant spymistress, **Seraphine Vey**, who was the boy's tutor and the nearest thing he had to a mother. His second-in-command was **Kultaka**, a silver-tongued Seer from the Core undercity. Kultaka was secretly a **Candlemouth**, born into a hidden cult that still worships the Empire, and the Emperor had sent him dreams since childhood: *find the stolen son.*

**~13 years ago: the Hundred Days.** Akulsa forged the Unity into something that could fight what was coming by conquering it. A hundred worlds fell in less than a hundred days. Among the dead: the royal fleet of **Seonha**, and with it Crown Prince **Jin-Ha Ananna**, Melonsi's elder brother.

**~12 years ago: the Betrayal at the Glasswood.** Over the crystal forests of **Vesper**, Kultaka turned half the fleet on Akulsa's flagship, the ***Last Hymn***, and fed its position to the Srov. Dying (he believed), Akulsa began the **Succession** on seven-year-old Tyranus. Kultaka's boarders and then the Srov broke in mid-rite. The rite was never completed: **the boy got some of Akulsa's memories, in shards.** The Srov took both. Kultaka fled with most of the Juggernauts. The galaxy was told Akulsa died.

**The Unremembering (12 years ago).** On **Ithrael**, Seer-Master **Ondine Saar** didn't execute them. She emptied them.
- Tyranus became **Dust of the Sun**: an orphan whose village burned, sold to slavers, freed by the Srov, hurt in a cave-in during his first trial. He woke in the temple medbay at seven. She built his early childhood from the memories of a dead boy (Teo Fenn of Calder) and anchored each false memory to a real place.
- Akulsa became **Ashe Dawnbringer**, an ex-soldier who came to the Srov late for training. Ondine kept him near the boy on purpose: a master and apprentice torn apart search for each other forever, and she wanted that bond regrown into something safe.
- **It didn't hold.** Ashe's nature surfaced: force first, ruthless, brilliant. **Five years ago** the Srov put him on "indefinite sabbatical." Fourteen-year-old Dust stowed away on his ship. The Srov eventually gave up asking for Dust back and named Ashe his Master. Ashe refuses the title: *"One must be a master, to be someone's Master."*

**The last five years.** Ashe and Dust as mercenaries. Dust "won" the *Galactic Companion* in a con on a fixed race; the gangers had let him win to frame him, and the ensuing escape left the crew wanted on that world for 13 counts of murder. Ashe bought **Deena Ateray** out of a crime lord's household, and the two of them persuaded the crime lord to forget her. **K'saabu** turned up aboard one day and never left.

**Now.** Kultaka keeps the rogue Vors fleets scattered and hidden. Their raids look like piracy, but they are **harvesting Seers**. For a decade he has been building **the Beacon** at **Hesper's Cinder**, a dead star near the Narrows. Burned Seers relight it into a lighthouse across the Black Between strong enough to bring the Emperor through. It needs a **wick**: a living vessel of the Emperor's blood. Kultaka has hunted Tyranus for twelve years. Everyone (Dust included, once he learns what he is) assumes Kultaka hunts the *heir of Akulsa*.

## 3. Why the player believes "I am Akulsa" (the misdirect, v2)
Dust is 19, and Akulsa fell 12 years ago, so Dust can't literally *be* Akulsa. The Succession rite solves this and makes the misdirect stronger:
1. Dust's memory fragments are **first person, adult, and commanding**: they are Akulsa's memories, poured into him during the broken rite. He sees himself give orders, conquer worlds, love Seraphine's loyalty, fight Kultaka.
2. Vors lore says a Grandmaster *passes into the heir*. The Unbowed Vors on Vesper kneel to Dust as **Akulsa reborn**.
3. Kultaka hunts him obsessively.
4. Dust's sword-flame is a "sooty" gold with a black core. Vors legend calls Akulsa's flame "a light like a hole in the sky."

**So the player believes:** *Akulsa's soul lives in me. I am him, reborn.* Act IV breaks it: Akulsa never died. He never needed an heir. He's been standing next to Dust for twelve years.

## 6. ACT I: The Princess of Seonha (Lumenhaven)

**Lumenhaven** is a Unity core world and a city-planet of neon towers over a drowned undercity. Its slums, **the Lowlight**, are rain, cables, noodle-stalls, holo-adverts, and arcades running on stolen power. Its districts: the Lowlight, the **Chrome Docks**, the **Starlight Rink** (a roller-disco ruined in the Hundred Days), the **B-Side** (drowned storm-drain arcades), the **Halo Spires** (rich uptown), and the **Unity Naval Command** in orbit.

**The situation.** Melonsi came to Lumenhaven to ask its fleet to join the Srov hunt. Her retinue was ambushed in the Halo Spires three weeks ago, betrayed by someone inside. All dead; her body never found. Unity Naval Command now has the *Galactic C* flagged as a Vors ship that crashed into its city. The crew are fugitives from page one.

### Beats
**I-1 Crash site (≈1h).** The warehouse belongs to **Mama Quell**, a Lowlight fence who wants damages for the hole in her wall. Deena needs parts to fix the *C* before Unity investigators trace it. Mama Quell will trade parts for a job. Introduces the slums, hub vendors (and **Pim Pockets**, the worst arms dealer in the Gyre), and the crew's "fugitives" status (Unity patrols you avoid or talk past).

**I-2 The Rink (≈1h).** The parts are in the ruined **Starlight Rink**, where scavengers led by the **Scavenger King** (junk-mech, disco-ball flail) are stripping it. The rink's hostess robot **ROXXY-7** has kept hosting parties for nobody for thirteen years. She joins as an optional companion: "Finally! Guests!"

**I-3 The princess's trail (≈1.5h).** Lowlight rumor: a "noble girl" has been seen in the **Velvet Room**, a Lowlight casino-lounge. The crew finds Melonsi **not captive**: she escaped the ambush alone, hid in the slums, and is working the Velvet Room's owner for passage off-world. She's dressed to be noticed, because a princess hidden in plain sight is a princess nobody expects. She doesn't want rescuing. She wants **her betrayer**. *"Rescue me later. First, you'll help me find the man who sold my guard to the dark."* Melonsi joins as a guest (her full recruitment is at I-7).

> **Melonsi on meeting Ashe** *(a long, appraising look)*: "So *you're* the one the Council couldn't keep. I expected someone older." *(Ashe's agelessness clue, first time.)*

**I-4 The B-Side (≈1.5h).** Melonsi's thread-sight follows the ambushers into the drowned arcades: a **Candlemouth** cell that has been snatching Seers off Lumenhaven's streets for the rogue Vors. **K'saabu's moment**: half the dungeon is underwater, and he goes where no one else can (solo underwater segment, his grunts subtitled). Boss: **Deacon Ninefingers**, who drowns Seers in candle wax. Evidence: the Candlemouths are selling captured Seers to "the Cardinal" (Kultaka). The betrayer's name: **Sir Halden Rook**, captain of Melonsi's own guard.

**I-5 First fragment (≈0.25h).** In the B-Side, a Vors war-memorial. Dust touches its mask. **Fragment 1 (first person, adult):** the bridge of a Juggernaut, gloved hands on the war-table, a hundred worlds lit red, and his own voice, deep and certain: *"A hundred worlds. Then we can begin."* Dust comes to on the floor with a nosebleed. Ashe is the one who catches him, and Ashe has gone pale.

**I-6 The Gala (≈1.5h).** Rook will sell Melonsi's location to Kultaka's buyer at the Founders' Gala in the Halo Spires. The crew infiltrates (social skill checks; Melonsi in her element; Deena in a borrowed gown demanding to be wined and dined). Then the lights die. **Kultaka appears** as a towering hologram, his cantors singing, and gives a sermon to the room: *"Lumenhaven. I've come to collect a debt your grandparents forgot they owed."* His assassins **the Three Graces** strike. Rook makes his move on Melonsi. Kultaka's holo finds Dust in the crowd, and its smile slows: *"Well. Look who's grown."* (Players: *he knows me.*) Boss: **the Three Graces + Rook** across a ballroom with collapsing light rigs.

**I-7 Rook (≈0.5h).** Rook, defeated, begs. Melonsi decides his fate (the player can influence it): **execute** / **hand to Unity** / **let him go** (he returns in Act III). Then Melonsi formally joins. She delivers the Srov's real message: the Council wants Ashe and Dust **back at Ithrael**. Ashe: "Called it."

**I-8 Running the blockade (≈0.75h).** The *C* is repaired. Unity Naval Command, rattled by the Gala, has locked down the planet. Escape: a ship-combat set piece (turn-based, small) through the orbital batteries that started the game, with a payoff: **K'saabu finally gets to commandeer a space station** (the Unity defense platform guarding the lane). The others go along with it because, this time, it's the only plan. Deena, furious: "Do *not* tell him this worked."

**Act I bosses:** Scavenger King, Deacon Ninefingers, the Three Graces + Sir Halden Rook.
**Act I end state:** the crew has Melonsi; Kultaka is real, and he knows Dust; the Srov want them home; Dust has an adult man's memory in his head.

**Act I to II bridge.** Before heading for Ithrael, the crew follows the Candlemouth ledgers: the stolen Seers were shipped to three places. Melonsi reads the list and stops: *"Calder. Gallowmarket. Vesper."* Dust: *"...those are mine."* They are the three places of his childhood: the village, the slave market, the place the Srov found him.

## 7. ACT II: Anchors (Calder, Gallowmarket, Vesper, any order)

Each world has five strands: **Anchor** (a false memory meets reality), **Harvest** (a Seer-abduction operation), a **companion quest**, an **ally** for the eventual Last Flotilla, and a **fragment** that unlocks a Weave ability. Level bands scale to the order chosen. Each world takes 3.5–4.5 hours.

### II-A Calder: "The Village That Didn't Burn"
Rain-sea world, stilt villages, bioluminescent tides, lantern-churches on sea-stacks.
- **Anchor.** Dust's oldest memory: **Merrow's Reach** burning, his mother pushing him into a boat. It's intact. **Marta Fenn** opens the blue door. He knows her face; she has never seen him. Her son **Teo** died in the Hundred Days, aged six, when Vors guns hit the shipyard crèche. His memories were given to the Srov at his funeral rite. **Dust's early childhood belonged to a dead boy.** Choice: *tell her / leave / stay for supper and let her believe.*
- **Harvest + companion quest (K'saabu).** The **Candlemouths** run the lantern-churches and drown Seers in the **Quiet Graves** under the sea. K'saabu's mystery is solved: he is a **Calder deep-dweller**, a species the Candlemouths enslaved to tend the graves. He fled years ago and climbed aboard the first ship he found. The cult's **Choirmother** owned him. Boss: **the Choirmother** (tide mechanics; she speaks in every voice she drowned). K'saabu's choice: kill her / free the other deep-dwellers first (harder fight, big ally) / drag her into the deep.
- **Fragment 2 (Akulsa's memory):** he orders the Calder bombardment himself. *"Fire."* Dust gave the order that killed Teo, or at least he remembers giving it. **Unlocks: Gravefire Brand.**
- **Clue:** Candlemouth scripture of **"the Son Who Was Carried Into Snow."**
- **Ally:** Lantern Keepers and Calder fishing fleet (plus deep-dwellers, if freed).

### II-B Gallowmarket: "The Market of Chains"
Asteroid casino-station, slave auction under a disco ball, neon-noir.
- **Anchor.** Dust "was sold here as a child." The ledgers show no such sale. They *do* show a Srov payment to crime lord **Velvet Jack** twelve years ago, *"for corroboration, should anyone ask."*
- **Deena's quest.** Velvet Jack is a business partner of the crime lord Ashe and Dust "persuaded," and he recognizes Deena. He wants her back, or at least wants to sell her. Deena faces that life again on her own terms. Comic betrayal from Jack mid-escape. Boss: **Velvet Jack & the Backup Singers** (crooning buffs; wreck the speakers, or Roxx out-sings him).
- **Harvest.** Kultaka's buyers purchase Seers at the Gilded Auction. Tonight's lot: a Vors prisoner in porcelain: **Seraphine Vey**. Free her (buy, gamble in "Jackpot Hymn", heist, or fight). She looks at Dust, and her hand goes to her mouth. *"...You have his eyes now. Oh, my little lord. What did they do to you?"* She kneels. (Players: *the Succession worked. I'm Akulsa.*) She knows the rite was never finished, and she says nothing.
- **Melonsi's quest.** Jack's trophy room holds the flight recorder of the **Seonha royal fleet**, her brother's flagship. Its last order bears the seal **AKULSA**. Melonsi: *"If Akulsa's soul really lives in anyone, I will cut it out of them."* She looks at Dust as she says it, and the romance starts to fray.
- **Sable** attacks in the auction dome (round two; she withdraws at 25%). *"You don't remember the oath. We were seven. We swore it in blood, and you don't even remember."*
- **Fragment 3:** Akulsa on a Tal Vorrun balcony, with child-Tyranus at his knee and Seraphine beside him. His voice: *"Love is the first thing they'll take from him. Teach him to make them pay for it."* (First person. Dust believes he is remembering his own past life, looking down at *some* child.) **Unlocks: Stitch.**
- **Ally:** the Rim Runners (smuggler gunships).

### II-C Vesper: "The Glasswood"
A forest of ringing crystal trees.
- **Anchor.** "The Srov rescued me in the Glasswood." At its heart lies the crystal-grown wreck of the ***Last Hymn***.
- **The Unbowed.** Old **Castellan Mordaine Hesk** and forty Vors veterans guard the wreck. They see Dust and **kneel**: *"The Grandmaster passes into the heir! Akulsa lives!"* Ashe stays outside: crystal gives him headaches.
- **Harvest.** Kultaka's excavators cut into the wreck for Akulsa's war-archive. Dungeon: the shattered, vertical *Last Hymn*. Boss: **the Reliquary Engine** (a dig-walker whose song shatters crystal floors).
- **Seraphine's quest:** Akulsa's chapel and her shrine of masks.
- **Fragment 4 (the big one, first person, Akulsa's):** the Betrayal at the Glasswood. Kultaka's guns, the bridge splitting, and "Dust" thrown down under burning girders. Kultaka through the fire: *"It's over, my lord. Give me the boy."* A small boy with a smoky-gold sword stands over him. Then the rite: Dust sees **his own child-face** through the master's eyes as the memories pour, Srov gold flame floods in, and Ondine's face appears. (This is the first fragment where Dust sees *Tyranus* from the outside. Players read it as "Akulsa looking at his heir, me." It is.)
- **Discoveries:** **Gravesong** (Tyranus's child-blade, reforged by Akulsa into a man's sword and left in the chapel). Akulsa's war-archive: the Narrows, Hesper's Cinder, and **the Ever-Throne**.
- **Ally:** the Unbowed and the Juggernaut ***Requiem Indomitable***.

### II-D Convergence (≈0.5h)
Kultaka is relighting a dead star with burned Seers to make a beacon across the Black Between. For whom? Ashe walks out of the briefing. On the hull that night: *"There's something past the edge, kid. Old. Hungry. Don't ask me how I know."*

## 8. ACT III: Ashfall (Ithrael)

The Srov temple on Ithrael: a monastery carved into a salt cliff, with resonance spires that sing in the wind. Dust hasn't been back since he was fourteen.

**III-1 Homecoming (≈1h).** The Srov receive them coldly, then warmly. **Ondine Saar**, small and kind and terrifying, welcomes Dust like a grandson. Old friends, old rivals, the medbay where he woke. Dust confronts Ondine about Teo Fenn. She doesn't deny it. *"I gave you a childhood. Its owner had no further use for it."* She won't tell him who he was: *"Some names are graves."*

**III-2 The Hollow (≈1h).** The Council demands Dust finish the trial he "failed" at seven: **the Hollow**, the cave. Inside, false memories bleed into salt crystal. At the bottom, frost spreads across the salt, and a tall figure stands waiting in black armour and a black cape, its **black mask** cut by a visor slit with **two white pinpricks** of light. It fights with his own moves. He cracks the mask and sees his own face behind it, older and colder. (Clue 2: the player reads it as "the darkness is me." In Act V, it's his father's mask.) His Weave deepens: **Seer Sight** at full strength.

**III-3 The Kindling (≈0.25h).** Before the Council, he kindles his sword at full strength for the first time in front of anyone. The sooty gold burns away and leaves **pure black**. Silence. Ondine: *"Eternity burns how it will."* (Clue 1, now unmissable.)

**III-4 The raid (≈1.25h).** That night Kultaka's Juggernauts fall on Ithrael. Fighting retreat through the temple: doors, bells, chokepoints. Sable leads the ground assault.

**III-5 The Hushword (≈0.5h).** Kultaka sings the old Vors oath across the burning temple, and Dust's eyes go black as memories flood him (**Fragment 5**: the oath sworn by two seven-year-olds, Tyranus and Sable). **Melonsi's betrayal:** the Council sent her to Lumenhaven with more than a message. She carries the **Hushword**, a trigger planted in Dust's mind that collapses him back into the wipe. Ondine orders her to speak it.
> **Branch (Melonsi influence and romance):**
> - **High:** she refuses. *"I've been told what to be my whole life. Not this."* Ondine turns to speak it herself.
> - **Low:** she speaks it. Dust collapses, and the party must hold a ring around his body (a hard fight). Afterward Melonsi says only: *"I was ordered."*

Either way, **Sable kills Ondine** before it matters. Dying in Dust's arms: *"Your name... is Tyranus. I took it. Forgive me..."* Her eyes go to Ashe. *"...and forgive him."*

**III-6 Kultaka (≈0.5h).** First true boss fight in the bell court. At 30% he **sacrifices his own cantors** to heal. He leaves with captured Srov Seers.

**III-7 Ashes (≈0.5h).** The temple burns. **Tyranus** is the name of Akulsa's apprentice; the Srov archives say so. Melonsi puts it together: Akulsa's heir is in her crew. She leaves (always), taking the Srov survivors and her fleet contacts. Whether she returns, and how, depends on influence. Seraphine is confronted: she knew. Forgive or condemn. Dust decides: *"If Akulsa lives in me, his fleet answers to me."* Destination: **Tal Vorrun**. Ashe argues hard against it, then goes: *"Someone's got to stand behind you, moron."*

## 9. ACT IV: The Hollow Crown (Tal Vorrun)

The Vors throneworld: endless storm, red lightning, cathedral-fortresses the size of mountains, drydocks of sleeping Juggernauts.

**IV-1 Landfall (≈1h).** Infiltrate through storm-catacombs. Kultaka's admiral **Halvard Grane**, an honorable old navy man disgusted by the sacrifices, can be met in disguise and swayed (sets up IV-5).

**IV-2 The Throne (≈0.75h).** The **Vors Throne** judges whoever sits. Dust sits. Vision: **a baby carried through snow by a masked man; a woman's body behind them; black flame on the horizon.** (Players: *Akulsa's origin? Mine?*) The throne neither accepts nor rejects him. Ashe won't enter the room.

**IV-3 Sable (≈1h).** Final duel in the Hall of Oaths. If Dust has Fragment 5, he can speak the oath back. Redeemed: guest for Act V. Killed: she dies saying the oath alone.

**IV-4 THE HOLLOW CROWN (≈0.75h).** Dust claims the throne before the Vors captains; the Juggernauts' bells ring for him. Kultaka walks in, slow-clapping, cantors singing.
> **KULTAKA:** "Oh, look at it. A boy in a borrowed crown. Is that what you think you are? The vessel? The heir? Akulsa *reborn*?" *(he laughs, delighted)* "The rite was never finished, child. You got his memories the way a dog gets scraps from the table. And the master himself..."
> *(He turns to the back of the hall.)*
> "Kneel, Vorsari. Your Grandmaster never died. He's been carrying the boy's luggage for twelve years."
> *(Kultaka strikes Ashe's sword. Its Srov enamel shatters, and black steel shows beneath: **Nightfall**. He speaks the Vors command-word.)*
> "Wake up, my old lord. **Akulsa.**"

Ashe's wipe breaks. His flame turns **starless white**. The captains kneel, and not to Dust. Kultaka boasts of his master's promise of forever and *"not this little spiral. The millions beyond."* He leaves them a summons: *"Come to Hesper's Cinder, Tyranus. Someone has waited a very long time to meet you."*

**IV-5 Turn the fleet (≈0.75h).** Hold the hall while Akulsa commands. If swayed, Grane turns the Juggernauts. If not, sabotage the drydock cradles.

**IV-6 The reckoning (≈0.75h).** Dust and Akulsa, alone.
- No apology for the Hundred Days: *"I'd do it again. And I'd hate myself again. Both are true."*
- About the Emperor, and no more: *"He's older than any star you've named. Strong in Eternity as I ever was, maybe stronger. I built the fleet for him. The Hundred were the price."*
- Why the Succession, why Dust? Akulsa hesitates: *"When this is done, I owe you something. If I tell you now, you won't come."*
- **Bond** set: Trust / Wary / Broken.
- **Deena**, loyal to Ashe above everything, has to decide whether she's loyal to *Akulsa*. Her scene with him is the quiet heart of the act.
- **Melonsi returns.** *High influence:* at the head of Seonha's fleet, sword drawn on Akulsa, her brother's killer. The player mediates. *Low influence:* she has sold the crew's route to the Unity as the price of her fleet, and arrives with Unity warships to arrest Akulsa. Fight or talk her down.

## 10. ACT V: The Black Between (Hesper's Cinder)

**V-1 The Last Flotilla (≈0.75h).** Synthwave hot-rods, Calder boats, Rim Runners, Srov singing ships, Seonha's royal fleet (if Melonsi came), and the Unbowed and Grane's Juggernauts under Akulsa's banner. Fleet command layer: flank assignments decide losses and epilogue survivals. **Deena flies the *Galactic C*** on the run that opens the Pyre's shield. K'saabu goes with her.

**V-2 The Pyre (≈1.5h).** A cathedral wrapped around a dead star, with caged Seers burning as candles. Freeing cages weakens the Beacon but costs turns and draws patrols. Companion finales along the climb.

**V-3 Kultaka (≈0.5h).** The Hall of the Wick. His chest-reliquary is empty: the Emperor keeps his heart. A three-phase fight. He falls. Then the floor ignites, and the Beacon lights, because **Dust is standing in it.** Kultaka, laughing on his knees: *"You lit it. You were always going to. Did you think I wanted you dead?"*

**V-4 The Emperor (≈0.25h).** The dead star opens. Frost races across the burning floor. **Emperor Voidreaper** steps through, and the room goes quiet: black armour, a black cape that doesn't stir, a **black mask with one horizontal slit and two white pinpricks** for eyes. He doesn't speak at first. He lifts one hand, the caged Seers' light drains into him, and Kultaka rises, healed. Kultaka keeps talking. The Emperor lets him.

### V-5 THE REVEAL (structure locked; wording in our own words)
> **EMPEROR:** "The thief never told you where you began."
> **DUST:** "He told me enough. I was taken from your worlds when he raided your Throne."
> **EMPEROR:** *(a deep bass laugh with a tinny metallic ring, like a bell struck underwater)* "Taken. Yes. And yet Eternity burns in you like a second sun. Black fire, child." *(He kindles his sword. Black.)* "Have you ever seen another blade burn that color? Did you think a thief found you by chance? Whose blood is that?"
> **DUST:** "He told me what you did. You cut down my mother. You gave my father to your dead gods."
> **EMPEROR:** "The truth, cut to fit. Your mother ran into the snow with you in her arms, and yes, I cut her down. And your father... I gave him to the Unsleeping long ago: his name, his warmth, every soft thing he was. What was left sat down on the Ever-Throne and never rose again."
> *(He steps closer. Frost crawls toward Dust's boots. The two white points of light hold his eyes.)*
> "You've seen this mask before. In the dark, under the salt. You struck it, and behind it you found your own face. Did you think that was a lie the cave told you?" *(beat)* "**You are my son, Tyranus. Blood of my blood.**"
> **DUST:** *(not looking away)* "Ashe. Tell me he's lying."
> **AKULSA:** *(long silence)* "...I can't."
> **AKULSA:** "I went past the edge when I was young and sure of everything. I found his Seers' prophecy: only his own blood can unmake him. So I stole his blood. Your mother held the gate so I could run. I told myself I was raising a weapon." *(beat)* "Then you learned to talk your way out of trouble you'd talked your way into, and I had to come and get you, every time. Somewhere in there it stopped being a weapon."
> **KULTAKA:** *(circling Akulsa, savoring it)* "Do you see, my old lord? This is what comes to those who kneel to the true Throne. *Forever.* And you get to watch."
> **EMPEROR:** *(offering his hand)* "Come home, my son. Leave the thief his ashes. Take my hand, and there will be no more dying, not for you, not for anything you choose to keep. Together we will become what the old gods only dreamed of being."

### V-6 Choice
- **Refuse** (canon). The player picks the name he answers with: *"My name is Dust."* or *"Tyranus."* → **final battle.**
- **Take his hand** → the Emperor demands the thief's death. Dust duels Akulsa 1v1, and Akulsa won't strike to kill. Ending: **The Ever-Heir**.

### V-7 THE FINAL BATTLE: Dust & Akulsa vs Kultaka & the Emperor
(Specified in `03_SYSTEMS.md` §8.4.)
1. **2v2.**
2. **"Forever":** at half health, the Emperor devours Kultaka. *"You promised me forever!"* / *"And you shall have it. Inside me."* The payoff to Kultaka's gloating.
3. **Blood:** the mask cracks. Only **gravefire** (Dust's flame, his father's blood) can harm him. Akulsa must keep Dust alive.
4. The Emperor's last strike goes for Dust. Akulsa takes it.

### V-8 Unmasking
The mask splits along the visor slit and falls. Beneath it: a face like Dust's, forty centuries older, as pale as frost. *"...she ran so fast. Through all that snow."* Ash. The Beacon dies.

### V-9 Akulsa's death
> **AKULSA:** "I took you to make a weapon. Failed at that, kid. Made a son instead. Worst plan I ever had." *(beat)* "Don't put my name on the stone. Put the other one. I liked him better."
