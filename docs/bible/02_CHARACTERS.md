# 02: CHARACTERS (v2)

> Every character is larger than life: a silhouette readable at PS1 resolution, a voice you could pick out of a crowd, a weapon that reflects them, and a combat kit that matches their personality (see `03_SYSTEMS.md` §6). Voice notes are binding for writers and for ElevenLabs.
> **Party in combat: 4.** Dust is always in. Ashe is in most story fights.

---

## 1. The starting crew (Jesse's canon)

### 1.1 Dust of the Sun ("Dust") / Tyranus
- **Age 19.** Woke in the Srov temple medbay on Ithrael at seven, "after a cave-in." Stowed away on Ashe's ship at fourteen. Mercenary since.
- **Personality:** a fledgling hero who is optimistic and incorruptible underneath a roguish, cocky charm. He's sure he can talk his way out of anything he talked his way into, and he's usually wrong; Ashe usually has to come and get him. He never leaves anyone behind. He jokes most when he's scared.
- **Look:** lean, quick, unruly dark hair with a white streak over a small, round, too-neat scar at the left temple (the mark of the Srov mind-seal, not a rock). A battered spacer jacket over Srov-cut undershirt (the last thing he kept from the temple), fingerless gloves, boots that have been resoled three times.
- **Sword: Kindling** (his own, made as a temple boy and rebuilt over the years). Slim, single-edged, eastern-style blade with a mismatched western knuckle-guard he added after a bar fight. Flame: **"sooty" gold with a black heart**. He thinks it's a quirk. Ashe has always told him not to kindle in public. At full strength (Act III) it burns **pure black**.
- **Seer gift: the Weave.** He sees threads and the gaps between them. Early: Push, Pull, Mend (basic healing). Grows with each memory fragment.
- **Voice:** young, warm, quick, wry; drops low and steady when he's dead serious, and those are the moments people remember.
- **Arc:** a good kid learns he was raised at a conqueror's side and carries an emperor's blood, and has to choose, with all of it in him, to still be the kid who never leaves anyone behind.

### 1.2 Ashe Dawnbringer / Vors Akulsa
- **Says he's in his early fifties. Doesn't look a day over 25.** (Strong Seers barely age. Clue.)
- **Personality:** ex-military, cold-eyed, charismatic, Machiavellian. A commander first, always. He solves problems with direct force and calls it efficiency. A grizzled, swaggering frontier-soldier manner: drawled, folksy, salty one-liners delivered with complete calm, and a chuckle when the shooting starts. He respects competence and nothing else, except, it turns out, Dust. He was in Srov training until his methods got him put on "indefinite sabbatical" five years ago.
- **On Dust:** called him a moron for leaving the temple; let him stay as long as he "kept up." Rejects the Srov's title of Master: *"One must be a master, to be someone's Master."* Comes to get him, every time, without ever admitting that's what he's doing.
- **Look:** tall, broad, a strong jaw, steel-grey eyes, close-cropped hair, old military coat with rank insignia torn off, a scar across the throat (Kultaka's blade), and very still hands.
- **Sword: Morningwake / Nightfall.** A heavy, two-handed, curved blade in white Srov enamel with a gold sunburst guard, chipped at one corner to show **black lacquer beneath**. In Act IV the enamel shatters: it is **Nightfall**, with a black blade and a crown-of-thorns guard. Flame: dawnfire gold (forced, flickery) → **starless white**.
- **Voice:** low, gravelly, unhurried drawl; a veteran's swagger and blunt, salty soldier-talk ("greaseballs," "scum paid to kill"); menace and charm in the same breath; chuckles under fire. Calls Dust "kid" and "moron," the second one fondly.
- **Combat identity:** the commander. **Dawnsight** shows enemy intents on the timeline. **Vigil** intercepts attacks on an ally. **Command**: once per fight he gives an ally an extra Move. After the reveal: **Nightfall stance**, devastating, paid for in his own HP.
- **Clues:** agelessness; the enamel chip; tactical brilliance "from the army"; flinching at Vors oath-song and the throne; he goes pale at Dust's first fragment.

### 1.3 Deena Ateray
- **Look:** blue-skinned alien, large expressive anime-style eyes, petite, bright coral hair tied up with a wrench through it, mechanic's overalls with a pistol holster and too many pockets, grease on her cheek like a beauty mark.
- **Personality:** cute, cantankerous, stubborn, lovable, dramatic. She flirts with both Dust and Ashe relentlessly, and anyone who wants a kiss has to wine and dine her first. Fiercely protective of her friends; loyal to Ashe above everything. Waxes poetic about every ship system right before it breaks. Certain they're all going to die, every single time.
- **Backstory:** a concubine-slave to a crime lord until Ashe bought her freedom and he and Dust "encouraged" the crime lord to forget her. Ship's mechanic of the *Galactic C*. She had never killed anyone before the opening.
- **Weapon:** a pistol she's customized past legality, **"Sweetheart."** Also a stun grenade she is told to save.
- **Voice:** bright, fast, melodramatic, sing-song when flirting, outraged otherwise.
- **Combat identity: fast and brittle.** Very high Speed (acts first), high **Perception** (best at **Search**), low HP. **Pass Turn** (cooldown): she gives her turn to another ally. Tricks and tools: stun grenade, overcharged shots, field repairs.
- **Arc:** from being owned to choosing. Gallowmarket makes her face her old life. Act IV makes her choose whether her loyalty to Ashe survives learning he's Akulsa. In Act V she flies the run that opens the Pyre.
- **Romance (with Dust):** the warm romance. It's built from banter and "you owe me dinner." It gets real at Gallowmarket, when he stands next to her while she faces Jack.

### 1.4 K'saabu
- **Look:** a 7-foot bipedal frog with mottled green-black skin, a gaping fang-lined maw, a heavy cannon slung under one arm (where anyone else would need a tripod), and a scrap of Deena's old scarf tied around his wrist.
- **Personality:** devoted to Deena like a guard dog. Worships Ashe because Ashe always beats him in a fight. Gives Dust a hard time: insults him, shoves him out of the way, leaves him in tight spots. That's love: he thinks Dust is green and will get himself killed unless he wises up. He believes every problem has a straightforward solution, usually "commandeer a space station."
- **Speech:** croaks and grunts. The crew understands him perfectly; everyone else thinks he's making noise. **UI gag:** when a crew member is listening, his lines are subtitled in brackets, e.g. *[Croak.] (We could take the station.)*. When strangers are the listeners, the subtitle just reads *[croak]*, and NPCs react to the noise.
- **Truth (Act II):** a **Calder deep-dweller**, enslaved by the Candlemouths to tend the Quiet Graves. He escaped and climbed aboard the first ship he found.
- **Combat identity: slow, heavy, collateral.** Slow Speed. Cannon is **always AoE**: great when there's nothing to break, a disaster near civilians, hostages, or delicate instruments. **Personal Shield**, **Taunt**, **Shove** (pushes any normal-sized person). Breathes underwater (solo underwater segments; can fight from water tiles).
- **Running gag paid off:** in Act I's finale he finally commandeers a space station, and it's the right call.

### 1.5 Princess Melonsi Ananna of Seonha
- **Look:** striking and beautiful, with East Asian–inspired royal styling from her world, Seonha. Space-opera flamboyant and functional at once: dramatic, figure-accentuating silk court wear cut for fighting, jeweled hair ornaments with gold pins, a high collar, and a flowing outer robe that announces "princess" from across a room. The women of this galaxy are every bit as larger-than-life as its men, and Melonsi is the proof. Always the most dressed person present, on purpose. *(Jesse has a reference image of her; get it into `/concept` when it arrives.)*
- **Personality:** huge presence. Direct, demanding, entitled, intelligent, cunning, and seductive when she chooses, which is a weapon she uses deliberately. A Srov-trained Seer. Hates being rescued. Hates being told what to do more.
- **Seer gift: Silk.** She sees the threads between people and can tie, pull, and cut them.
- **Sword: Eunha ("Silver River").** A narrow, jeweled straight sword with long silk tassels; a ribbon of dawnfire unspools from the blade like a scarf.
- **Backstory:** her elder brother, Crown Prince Jin-Ha, died with Seonha's fleet in the Hundred Days. The order bore Akulsa's seal. She took the Srov's side out of grief and fury.
- **Secret:** the Srov Council gave her the **Hushword**. She was sent to watch Dust and, if his memories surfaced, to silence them.
- **Voice:** precise, regal, unhurried; amused contempt that warms by degrees into something real.
- **Combat identity: the puppeteer.** **Silk** links two units: ally-ally shares healing and damage; enemy-enemy shares damage and status; **Pull** drags a linked enemy. Charm (an enemy skips its action). Swordplay: fast, precise. Fragile at range.
- **Arc and romance (with Dust):** the dangerous romance. Sparks at first meeting ("You're the *rescue*?"). She learns to want something nobody ordered her to want. Gallowmarket (her brother's recorder) cracks it. Act III (Hushword) tests it. Act IV (she has Akulsa at sword-point) decides it.
- **Ashe and Melonsi:** she flirts with him at first, as a test. He sees through her instantly, and they respect each other like two blades.

### 1.6 The ship: the *Galactic C*
A *Galactic Companion*-class civilian passenger transport. Someone scratched off "ompanion" and painted "hampion," so the hull reads ***Galactic Champion***. The crew call her the *Galactic C*. Rounded 80s design, chrome trim, flip-up headlights, a passenger lounge turned into a crew mess, a cassette deck wired into the PA. **Designed to glide unpowered** in an emergency (passenger safety regulations), which is the whole plan in the opening. Won by Dust in a con on a fixed race; the con was itself a set-up by the gangers who owned her, which ended with 13 counts of murder and a planet they can never return to.

## 2. Companions who join later

### 2.1 ROXXY-7 ("Roxx"), optional, Act I
A pink-and-teal roller-disco hostess robot from the ruined Starlight Rink, rebuilt with crooked armor plates. She's been hosting parties for nobody for thirteen years. Talks like an 80s radio DJ; slows like a dying tape when hurt. **Combat:** deploys speakers that broadcast tracks (positional auras that affect allies *and* enemies). **Quest:** keep or delete the memory of the night the Rink was hit.

### 2.2 Seraphine Vey, Act II
- ~40, Vors Seer, Akulsa's spymistress, and **child-Tyranus's tutor and surrogate mother**. Porcelain masks (Grief, Mirth, Wrath, Silence); the left half of her face is burn-scarred.
- Recruited from the Gallowmarket auction. She recognizes him immediately: *"Oh, my little lord."* She knows he's Tyranus and that Akulsa may be alive, and says nothing, because she believes in Akulsa's war and wants him whole again.
- **Combat:** mask swapping: drain, decoys, burst, silence. The most flexible character, and fragile.
- **Arc:** a mother who lost a child to the Srov and finds him grown and gentle. Does she want Tyranus back, or is Dust enough? Not a romance.

### 2.3 Sable Nox (rival, possible Act V guest)
Kultaka's apprentice; Tyranus's oath-sibling. They swore in blood at seven. A white porcelain mask with one painted black tear; twin sickle-swords, **Mercy** and **Mourning**. She believes he chose the Srov and abandoned her. Duels: Gallowmarket, Ithrael, Tal Vorrun. Redeemable by speaking the oath back to her.

## 3. Villains

### 3.1 Vors Kultaka, the Cardinal of Ash
2.2 m, gaunt, cathedral armor of black iron and stained glass, an **empty glass reliquary** over his chest (the Emperor holds his heart), a crown of candle-tipped spikes, and four masked **cantors** who follow him singing. Sword **Penitence**: a colossal glass-toothed cleaver trailing incense from chained censers; wrathfire with a green core. Silken, operatic, funny, cruel, terrified of dying. Musical motif: the Vors march with Empire bells hidden under it. Devoured by his master.
> *Spelling note: Jesse's message uses both "Kultaka" and "Skultaka." The bible uses **Kultaka** until Jesse confirms.*

### 3.2 Emperor Voidreaper
A tall, still figure, taller than any man but not a giant, in close-fitting **black armour** of smooth, unornamented plates and a heavy **black cape** that falls straight to the floor, unbroken and unmoving even in wind. His mask is **black**, smooth, and featureless except for one **narrow horizontal visor slit**, and in that slit two **white pinpricks** of light that never blink. Wherever he stands, **frost** creeps out across the floor (the snow of the Pale follows him: the snow his son was carried through). No candles, no ornament, no theatre: Kultaka is the overblown cathedral; the Emperor is the **quiet** that comes after it.
- **Presence:** a quiet kind of terror. He rarely moves and never hurries; he speaks softly and seldom; when he does something terrible, he does it without raising his voice. He lets Kultaka monologue, and then says one sentence that ends the room.
- **Sword: the Last Word Spoken.** Vast, plain, ancient, the only unornamented blade in the game, because there's no self left in him to express. **Gravefire** (black with a sick green rim).
- **Voice:** deep bass with a tinny metallic ring; slow; when he laughs, it goes on too long.
- Seen in person only in Act V.
